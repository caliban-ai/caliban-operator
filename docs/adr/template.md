## Context

What is the problem or force that requires a decision? Describe the situation,
constraints, and the options considered — enough that a reader who wasn't there
understands *why a decision was needed*. State the alternatives you weighed.

## Decision

The decision, in the active voice: "We will …". Be specific about what is and isn't
included. Link to the relevant design doc, spec, or code if one exists. If this ADR
supersedes or amends an earlier one, say so here and annotate both sides (this
header and the older ADR's row in `README.md`).

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what becomes easier or safer as a result — the upside you're buying.
- **Negative:** what becomes harder, the cost or constraint you accept, the follow-up
  work it implies.
- **Revisit if:** the condition or signal that would make this decision worth
  reopening — what you'd watch for that says "this trade-off no longer holds."
